How to Maintain a Resin Bound Driveway: Complete Care Guide

Published 01 September 2026 · Bournemouth Driveway Pros Guides

Quick answer: A resin bound driveway needs light sweeping every few weeks, an annual low-pressure wash, prompt attention to any oil or fuel spills, and a re-coat roughly every 8–12 years to maintain its full 20–25 year lifespan.

Regular Light Care

A soft-bristle broom every few weeks clears leaves, dirt and debris before they can work into the surface. This alone prevents most of the discolouration issues homeowners see over time.

Annual Deep Clean

Once a year, a low-pressure wash (see our pressure washing guide for the safe settings) removes ground-in dirt and any early moss or algae before it becomes established.

Dealing With Spills and Stains

Oil, fuel or fat spills should be blotted (not rubbed) and cleaned promptly with a mild detergent — resin bound is more resistant to staining than block paving, but quick action still gives the best result.
