Why Choose Gravel

Affordable, Fast and Naturally Draining

Gravel is the budget-friendly, quick-turnaround choice — ideal if you want a smart-looking driveway without the cost of a bound surface.

  • The most affordable driveway surface, typically from £30/m²
  • Fast installation — often completed in a single day
  • Naturally permeable — excellent drainage, SUDS-friendly
  • Wide choice of stone colours and sizes to suit your property
  • Easy to top up or repair yourself over time
Our Process

How We Install Your Gravel Driveway

1

Excavation

Excavate to the correct depth to accommodate the sub-base and gravel layer.

2

Weed Membrane & Sub-base

Lay a geotextile membrane and compacted sub-base to stop weeds and prevent the gravel sinking.

3

Edging

Install edging (timber, metal or block) to keep the gravel contained and neat.

4

Gravel Laying

Spread and rake your chosen stone to an even depth, then compact for a stable, comfortable surface.
