Hidden Driveway Costs Nobody Tells You About

Published 19 August 2026 · Bournemouth Driveway Pros Guides

Quick answer: The most commonly missed driveway costs are excavation and old-surface removal (£300–£800+), skip hire, dropped kerb applications (typically £400–£1,500 depending on council and works), drainage upgrades for SUDS compliance, and access charges for narrow or restricted sites.

Excavation and Removal

If your existing driveway needs breaking out and taking away before the new surface goes in, that’s additional cost most bare per-m² prices don’t include. Ask any installer explicitly whether removal of the old surface, and disposal, is included in the headline figure.

Dropped Kerbs and Drainage

If you need a new or altered dropped kerb, that’s a separate council application and cost on top of the driveway itself — see our dropped kerb guide for typical figures. Likewise, non-permeable driveways over 5m² may need extra drainage work to meet SUDS rules, covered in our permeable driveways guide.

Access and Site Conditions

Restricted access for machinery, a long carry distance from the road, or ground that needs extra excavation depth because of poor drainage can all add cost that only becomes clear at a proper site visit — another reason to avoid quotes given without one.
