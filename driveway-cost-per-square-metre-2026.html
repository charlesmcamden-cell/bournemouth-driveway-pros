Driveway Cost Per Square Metre 2026: Every Material Compared

Published 21 August 2026 · Bournemouth Driveway Pros Guides

Quick answer: 2026 driveway costs per m²: gravel £30–£50, tarmac £40–£70, block paving £60–£100, resin bound £70–£110. Multiply by your driveway’s area for a rough installed cost, though groundwork and access can move the final figure.

The Four Materials, Side by Side

Gravel: £30–£50/m². Tarmac: £40–£70/m². Block paving: £60–£100/m². Resin bound: £70–£110/m². These figures assume standard groundwork on a reasonably accessible site — see the individual tarmac, block paving, resin bound and gravel pages for size-based pricing tables.

Working Out Your Driveway's Area

Measure the longest length and widest width in metres and multiply them for a rough area — irregular shapes are best measured in sections. Most single-car driveways are around 25–30m², doubles 45–60m².

Why Per-m² Prices Are Only a Starting Point

Per-m² pricing is useful for comparing materials, but excavation, drainage and access all affect the final number — which is why we always recommend a free site visit over a phone estimate.
