Winter Driveway Care: Protecting Against Frost and Salt Damage

Published 03 September 2026 · Bournemouth Driveway Pros Guides

Quick answer: Avoid rock salt on resin bound driveways, as it can degrade the resin over time — use a resin-safe de-icer instead. Block paving and tarmac tolerate standard rock salt better, but all surfaces benefit from clearing standing water before a freeze to reduce frost damage.

Which Surfaces Are Most at Risk

Resin bound is the most sensitive to standard rock salt, which can gradually break down the resin binder over repeated winters. Block paving and tarmac are more tolerant, though excessive salt use isn’t ideal for any surface long-term.

De-Icing the Right Way

Use a resin-safe de-icer on resin bound driveways, and apply any salt or de-icer sparingly and evenly rather than piling it in one area.

Preventing Frost Damage

Standing water that freezes and expands is the main cause of frost damage on any surface — make sure drainage is clear of leaves and debris going into winter, and address any standing water issues (see our drainage guide) before the cold sets in.
