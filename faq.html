Most residential driveways take 1–3 days depending on size and material — gravel and tarmac are typically fastest, block paving and resin bound take slightly longer due to base preparation.

Under UK ‘permitted development’ rules, you don’t need permission if the new surface is permeable (gravel, permeable block paving, or resin bound on a SUDS-compliant base) or drains onto your own garden. Impermeable surfaces over 5m² draining to the street generally do require permission — we’ll advise during your free site visit.
