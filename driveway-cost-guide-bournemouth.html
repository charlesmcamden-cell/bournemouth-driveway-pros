Driveway Cost Guide: Bournemouth Prices 2026

How much does a driveway cost in Bournemouth? Here’s a full, honest breakdown by material, size and the factors that move the price — plus how to get an exact figure.

Quick answer: most Bournemouth driveways cost between £30 and £110 per m² installed. Gravel is the cheapest option, resin bound is typically the most expensive. A typical single-car driveway (approx. 25–30m²) costs roughly £900 to £3,300 depending on material.

What Affects the Price

7 Factors That Change Your Driveway Cost

Size

The single biggest factor — price is usually quoted per m², so larger driveways cost more in total but often slightly less per m².

Existing surface removal

Breaking up and removing an old concrete, tarmac or block driveway adds to groundwork time and skip/tip costs.

Access

Restricted access for machinery or materials (narrow side passages, no direct road access) can increase labour time.

Groundwork & drainage

Soft ground, poor drainage or the need for a soakaway/permeable base adds cost but is essential for a long-lasting surface.

Edging & kerbing

Decorative edging, kerbing or a border in a contrasting material adds a premium finish — and a modest cost.

Pattern & design

Herringbone block paving or multi-colour resin blends take longer to lay than a single, uniform surface.

Dropped kerb

If you need a new dropped kerb, factor in the BCP Council permission and installation cost separately.
