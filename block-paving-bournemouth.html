Why Choose Block Paving

Style, Strength and Easy Repairs

Block paving is the top choice for homeowners who want their driveway to genuinely enhance the look of their property.

  • Huge choice of colours, patterns and block styles
  • Individual blocks can be lifted and replaced — no need to resurface the whole drive
  • Adds noticeable kerb appeal and can boost property value
  • Permeable block paving available to meet SUDS drainage rules
  • Extremely durable when laid on a properly compacted base
Our Process

How We Install Your Block Paving Driveway

1

Excavation & Sub-base

Excavate to depth and lay a compacted MOT Type 1 sub-base for long-term stability.

2

Edge Restraints

Install concrete or block edging to lock the pattern in place and prevent spreading.

3

Laying the Blocks

Lay blocks in your chosen pattern (herringbone, basketweave, stack bond) on a sand bed.

4

Compacting & Jointing

Vibrate the surface, brush in jointing sand, and complete a final wash-down and inspection.
