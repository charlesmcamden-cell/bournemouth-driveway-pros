Why Choose Tarmac

Durable, Practical and Great Value

Tarmac is the go-to choice for homeowners who want a smooth, hard-wearing surface without the premium price tag of block paving or resin.

  • Cost-effective — typically the second cheapest option after gravel
  • Fast to install, often finished within 1–2 days
  • Smooth, comfortable surface for cars, bikes and pushchairs
  • Highly durable — lasts 20–30 years with proper maintenance
  • Simple to repair and resurface if damaged
Our Process

How We Install Your Tarmac Driveway

1

Excavation

Remove the existing surface and excavate to the correct depth for a stable sub-base.

2

Sub-base & Edging

Lay and compact a MOT Type 1 sub-base, then install edging to contain the new surface.

3

Tarmac Laying

Apply and roll the tarmac in layers for a smooth, durable, properly compacted finish.

4

Finishing

Line marking (if required), clean-up and final inspection with you before we leave.
